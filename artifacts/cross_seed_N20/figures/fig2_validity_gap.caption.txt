Figure 2. Per-seed REALIZED VALIDITY RATE of the A = −1 group at L1-oracle,
one marker per seed, for each of the 12 cells in a fixed (topology, family,
regime) order — cells are never re-ordered by value, so the comparison reads
against the labelled axis. This figure plots ONE GROUP'S LEVEL: it is neither
ValidityGap_g(c) (believed − realized, per group) nor ValidityDisp(c)
(realized A = −1 − realized A = +1); no cross-group difference is drawn.
Colour carries the functional family and marker shape carries the topology,
which the axis order also encodes. WHAT THE BANDS DO. Every cell has a mode at
validity ≈ 1.0, and every one of the 12 cells reaches 1.000 on at least one
seed. What differs is the LOWER TAIL. 63 of 240 (cell, seed) observations fall
below 0.9, and 10 fall below 0.1 — a near-total failure of recourse for that
group on that seed. Those 10 collapse observations are confined to 5 of the 12
cells: collider · linear · effect-modifying 1/20; collider ·
nonlinear-Gaussian · additive 3/20; collider · nonlinear-Gaussian ·
effect-modifying 3/20; chain · nonlinear-Gaussian · additive 2/20; chain ·
nonlinear-Gaussian · effect-modifying 1/20. Per topology, the count of
observations below 0.9 is triangle 5/80, collider 29/80, chain 29/80, and the
lowest single value is triangle 0.566, collider 0.000, chain 0.027. NO
TOPOLOGY IS FREE OF THE LOWER TAIL. The triangle is the mildest — no collapse
seed anywhere, and 3 of its 4 cells hold every seed at or above 0.96 — but its
linear additive cell still puts 5 seeds between 0.566 and 0.851, so "no switch
on the triangle" is not what these data show. THE CHAIN, FIRST APPEARANCE. The
chain was added to the grid after the early look and has never appeared in a
validity figure; its read here is uncorroborated by any prior look. Its
low-tail count ties the collider for the largest of the three (29 of 80
observations below 0.9), its linear additive cell is the single most
tail-heavy on the grid (11/20 seeds below 0.9), and its two nonlinear-Gaussian
cells contain collapse seeds. WHY NO MEAN OR SD IS DRAWN. At the N = 6 early
look this distribution read as near-binary with a sparse middle. At N = 20 the
middle is POPULATED (53 observations between 0.1 and 0.9), so the accurate
description is a heavy mode at ≈ 1.0 with a long left tail rather than two
clean modes. The conclusion is unchanged: a mean ± SD would report a centre
where little mass sits, so the moments are omitted and every seed is shown.
Validity is a proportion and so is scale-free across topologies, but the
cross-topology reading remains pattern-only per PS-8. N = 20 seeds per cell
(PS-1's pinned ceiling). This figure is descriptive: H2's verdict is recorded
at LD-3 and in t4b_validity_gap.md, not here. There are no standard errors or
confidence intervals on this figure: PS-9 re-tasks SD as descriptive
dispersion, and a formal interval would over-promise precision this structure
lacks (the inference stance).
